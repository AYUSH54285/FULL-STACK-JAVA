Big game player: Why Rohit Sharma is coach Mahela Jayawardene's favourite

Mumbai Indians head coach Mahela Jayawardene was all praise for Rohit Sharma for his fantastic knock of 81 runs in the Eliminator of the Indian Premier League 2025. Rohit opened the innings alongside Jonny Bairstow in the match against Gujarat on Friday, and set the tone with a brilliant 84-run partnership.

It was a different innings from Rohit as the batter took time to get to terms with the pace of the ball. Rohit, after initially struggling and having two of his catches put down, shook the rustiness off and hit back at the GT bowlers, scoring a 28-ball half-century. He did not stop there and slammed a lovely knock of 81 runs, which helped Mumbai to get to a daunting total of 228 runs at the Mullanpur Stadium.

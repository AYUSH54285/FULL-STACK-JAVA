Online e-commerce shopping has come a long way. Initially limited to a handful of lifestyle and essential commodities, the online retail world has transformed dramatically over the last few decades. Until a couple of years ago, nobody would have imagined buying vehicles through e-commerce platforms and now even that is possible.

Flipkart, one of the largest e-commerce platforms in India, got the stone rolling with prominent two-wheeler brands like Hero, Bajaj, and TVS selling their respective products on the e-commerce website. The latest additions to this list are KTM and Triumph, both brands operating in India through Bajaj Auto’s partnership.
